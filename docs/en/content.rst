Distributed E-Maj distribution content
======================================

Once :doc:`installed <install>`, a Distributed E-Maj version contains the following files.

+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Files                                       | Usage                                                               |
+=============================================+=====================================================================+
| AUTHORS.md                                  | Who are the authors                                                 |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CHANGES.md                                  | Release notes                                                       |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CONTRIBUTING.md                             | How to contribute to the project                                    |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| dist_emaj.control                           | Extension control file for PostgreSQL's extensions management       |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| LICENSE                                     | E-Maj license information                                           |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Makefile                                    | Install/uninstall description for the make command                  |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| META.json                                   | Technical data for PGXN                                             |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| README.md                                   | Reduced extension documentation                                     |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SECURITY.md                                 | Process in case of vulnerability discovery                          |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| client/distEmaj.pl                          | *Perl* client to start/stop clusters and set distributed marks      |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| client/distEmajRollback.pl                  | *Perl* client to spawn distributed rollbacks                        |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| doc/dist_emaj.<version>_pres.en.odp         | English version of the E-Maj presentation (OpenDocument)            |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| doc/dist_emaj.<version>_pres.en.pdf         | English version of the E-Maj presentation (PDF)                     |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| doc/dist_emaj.<version>_pres.fr.odp         | French version of the E-Maj presentation (OpenDocument)             |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| doc/dist_emaj.<version>_pres.fr.pdf         | French version of the E-Maj presentation (PDF)                      |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
| sql/dist_emaj--<version>.sql                | Extension installation script                                       |
+---------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------+
